keryx privacy policy

keryx is a personal email client used only by its author, Alec Mitchell-Thomson. It has no other users.

  • What it accesses: the author's own Gmail messages and labels, through the Gmail API scope gmail.modify.
  • Where data goes: messages are stored only on the author's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared, or sent to third parties. The author may use an AI assistant on his own account to read or summarise his mail.
  • Deletion: keryx never permanently deletes mail. Revoking access at myaccount.google.com/permissions stops it at once, and deleting the local database removes the copy.
  • Google API data: use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
